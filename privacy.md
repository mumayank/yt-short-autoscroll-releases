# Hey, about your privacy

I made this app because I'm lazy. 🪥 I watch Shorts while brushing my teeth and I hate scrolling with a wet, foamy hand. If that sounds like you, you'll probably like this. It's free for life. Use it as much as you want.

Now the serious bit, said simply: I don't want your data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracker. The APK does not call home. Nothing leaves your phone.

I'm also not watching the Short with you. I never see the video. I don't grab the title, the channel, the description, how long it is, or any of that. The app reads the player's progress control (its id and time, not a screenshot and not the bar's color), so it can swipe when the clip is done. If a broadcast is still live, it also looks for the on-screen LIVE / watching / chat labels so it can skip those, because they never end. It does not read captions to decide. The buttons show up when a Short is on screen. They vanish when it isn't. It does not poke around your messages, files, passwords, or other apps.

Pause auto-scroll, switch the accessibility service off, or uninstall. After that, nothing of mine is running. Promise. 🫶
