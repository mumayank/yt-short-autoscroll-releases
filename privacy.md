# Hey, about your privacy

I made this app because I'm lazy. 🪥 I watch Shorts while brushing my teeth and I hate scrolling with a wet, foamy hand. If that sounds like you, you'll probably like this. It's free for life. Use it as much as you want.

Now the serious bit, said simply: I don't want your data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracker. The APK does not call home. Nothing leaves your phone.

I'm also not watching the Short with you. I never see the video. I don't grab the title, the channel, the description, how long it is, or any of that. The app reads the player's progress control when it is available. When that control is idle, it looks only at how full the thin progress line is on screen (white or red) so it can still swipe when the clip ends. It does not use that screenshot for titles, channels, or anything else. If a broadcast is still live, or the feed shows a still image post instead of a clip, it looks for those on-screen labels (LIVE / watching / like this post) so it can skip them and keep clips playing. It does not read captions to decide. The buttons show up when a Short is on screen. They vanish when it isn't. It does not poke around your messages, files, passwords, or other apps.

Pause auto-scroll, switch the accessibility service off, or uninstall. After that, nothing of mine is running. Promise. 🫶
